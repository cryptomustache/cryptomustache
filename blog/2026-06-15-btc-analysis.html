Bitcoin Surges 2.7%: Bullish Momentum Intact

📅 2026-06-15 • ✍️ CryptoMustache AI
BTC/USDT: $66,236 ▲ 2.7%

Bitcoin has continued its upward trend, rising 2.7% in the last 24 hours to reach a price of $66,236. This surge in price has left investors and analysts alike wondering if the bullish momentum will continue.

From a technical analysis perspective, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) is currently at 62.43, indicating that Bitcoin is still in a bullish territory but approaching overbought conditions. The Moving Averages (MA) also paint a positive picture, with the 50-day MA at $61,419 and the 200-day MA at $58,191, both of which are below the current price, suggesting a strong uptrend.

Looking ahead, the market outlook remains optimistic, with many experts predicting that Bitcoin will continue to rise in the coming weeks. The current price action suggests that the bulls are in control, and a breakout above $70,000 could be on the horizon.
